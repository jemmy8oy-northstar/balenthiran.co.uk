# Earn Your Drinks

**Status**: Idea (Backlog)
**Category**: Just for Fun
**Platform**: Cross-platform Mobile (TBD)

## Concept
A social fitness hybrid app designed to balance lifestyle enjoyment with physical activity. The core loop is simple: log your drinks and "earn" them back through exercise.

## Context
- **Origin**: Built as a conceptual request for a friend who wanted a way to gamify fitness in a social context.
- **Tone**: Lighthearted, competitive, and social.
- **Goal**: To create a low-friction way to encourage movement without sacrificing social experiences.

## Core Features
1. **Drink Logging**: Real-time logging of alcoholic or non-alcoholic beverages.
2. **Exercise Conversion**: Automatic or manual conversion of drinks into physical debt (e.g., 1 pint = 5km run/cycle).
3. **Debt Tracking**: A clear dashboard showing your current "balance".
4. **Community Feed**: Social features to see what friends are drinking and how they are paying it off.

## Technical Goals
- **Offline First**: Primary functionality should work without a stable connection for use in pubs/venues.
- **Iterative Testing**: Designed for rapid prototyping and user feedback.
